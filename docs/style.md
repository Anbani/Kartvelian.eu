# Style

- **Audience:** non-native learners, in English. Page one assumes no Mkhedruli; by module 3 they read it.
- **Transliteration:** italics after every new Mkhedruli word, in parentheses or its own column:
  **გამარჯობა** (*gamarjoba*). Mkhedruli alone once introduced on the page.
- **One scheme per page:** Georgian national romanization (`gh kh ts ch zh sh dz j`). Ejectives with an apostrophe
  (`k' p' t' ts' ch' q'`): all or none on a page. IPA in slashes: `/ɡɑmɑrd͡ʒɔbɑ/`. Svan, Megrelian, Laz: same, plus
  their own letters (Svan vowel marks, Megrelian ჸ ჷ, Laz ჲ, Laz Latin).
- **Equal standing:** Svan, Megrelian, Laz are languages, not Georgian dialects. Compare to help, never to rank.
- **Tone:** warm, exact, short. One explanation, three examples. Tables for paradigms.
- **Sources:** cite the grammar, dictionary or speaker; for Svan, Megrelian, Laz name the variety (Upper/Lower Svan,
  Atina/Hopa Laz).
